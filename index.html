About Me

I am an AI safety researcher and full-stack developer with experience leading applied AI projects from research design through deployment. My work combines model evaluation, behavioural analysis, data infrastructure, and clear communication for technical and non-technical stakeholders.

I am particularly interested in how AI systems behave under changing deployment conditions and how rigorous evaluation can surface safety-relevant capabilities and failure modes. I completed BlueDot Impact's Biosecurity Fundamentals course and am applying this perspective to AI×Bio research and public-interest tooling.
